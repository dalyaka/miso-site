Privacy Policy

Effective July 28, 2026

Miso is built local-first. There are no accounts, no cloud storage and no analytics. Your health data is processed on your iPhone and Apple Watch and never leaves your devices. This policy explains exactly what the app touches and what it does not.

What Miso reads

With your permission, Miso reads the following from Apple Health:

  • Sleep (including sleep stages)
  • Heart rate and resting heart rate
  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Respiratory rate and wrist temperature
  • Steps, active energy and workouts

Miso writes one thing back to Apple Health: breathing minutes, saved as mindful sessions. You can revoke any of these permissions at any time in the Health app (Profile → Privacy → Apps → Miso).

What Miso stores, and where

Everything Miso derives from your data (daily scores, baselines, insights, journal entries, your pet's state) is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private container. A small summary is shared with Miso's own widgets and your paired Apple Watch so they can show the same picture. That transfer happens through Apple's frameworks between your own devices; no server of ours is involved, because we do not have one.

What Miso does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting services
  • No advertising and no tracking of any kind
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties
  • No network connections: the app does not talk to any server
  • Health data is never used for marketing

Journal entries

The journal lets you tag your day (for example stress, caffeine, illness or cycle). These tags are sensitive by nature. They are stored only on your device, are never shared anywhere, and do not appear on share cards you export.

Subscriptions

Miso offers an auto-renewable subscription handled entirely by Apple through your App Store account. We never see your payment details. Purchases are validated on-device through Apple's StoreKit.

Your data, your control

  • Export. Settings → Data & privacy → Export my data gives you a complete JSON copy of everything Miso has computed.
  • Erase. Settings → Data & privacy → Erase everything deletes all of Miso's data from your device. Mindful sessions Miso saved to Apple Health stay in Apple Health, where you can delete them yourself.

Because nothing is stored off-device, deleting the app (plus the erase step above) removes everything.

Children

Miso is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. It collects no personal information from any user; all processing stays on the device.

Not a medical device

Miso is for general wellness. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any disease or condition, and it is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Since the app has no server, a policy change can never retroactively affect data, because we never had any.
