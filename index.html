From your own nights

She learns what helps your sleep

Coffee after lunch? A late meal? A stressful Tuesday? Tag your day in ten seconds each morning, and Miso slowly works out what actually helps you and what hurts. She compares you only to your usual. Never to other people, never to population norms.
